Silicone tubing provides a high-grip covering for rollers:

* Suitable for FRC roller mechanisms
* High-grip 45A silicone
* Consistent 1.5mm wall thickness
* Supplied in 1.5m lengths
* 4 outside diameters available

:::note

Low-pressure compressed air can help with installation. Start one end of the silicone sleeve on the tube, then slowly feed air into the other end to expand the sleeve slightly as you slide it into place. Wear safety glasses, avoid excessive air pressure, and keep the sleeve and tube securely controlled so they cannot suddenly separate or eject.

:::
