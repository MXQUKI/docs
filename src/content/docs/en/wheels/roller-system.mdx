The NICE Roller System is a modular solution for FRC intakes, indexers, and shooters. It includes roller hardware and high-grip surface materials for picking up, guiding, and moving game pieces.

**The metal Roller System is designed for strength, precision, and reuse.** Its separate components can be assembled as an end plug alone, an end plug with one pulley, or an end plug with two pulleys. With two end-plug sizes, there are 6 configurations to suit different roller diameters, drive layouts, and space constraints. Shared components also simplify spare-parts stocking.

**Silicone grip tape adds a wear-resistant, high-friction surface to a roller.** It helps game pieces engage consistently during pickup, guiding, and transfer. NICE offers this textured tape as a cost-effective alternative to similar roller grip materials.

### Features

* Modular parts let you choose the pulley configuration
* High-strength aluminum alloy construction
* Locknut housed inside the shaft
* Accommodates 10mm-wide timing belts
* Fits two round-tube sizes

### Configurations

<DocImage src="/assets/docs/wheels/RollerSystem.png" alt="Roller system configurations" variant="wide" />

End plugs fit two roller tube sizes: _**Φ35mm\*Φ31mm**_ (NICE-07-05-001) and _**Φ50mm\*Φ46mm**_ (NICE-07-05-002). All roller pulleys have 22T and can be stacked to suit the drive layout.
