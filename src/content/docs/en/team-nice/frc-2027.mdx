Supporting Teams. Building Better Robots.

Thank you for applying to the Team NICE FRC Team Support Program 2027. The program supports FRC teams that actively participate in FIRST Robotics Competition, pursue engineering innovation, and contribute to the robotics community.

Selected teams will receive:

- A NICE Robotics shopping voucher worth RMB 1,000;
- Support with selecting and using NICE Robotics products;
- Team NICE Supported Team partnership status;
- Priority opportunities for new product testing, technical exchange, and content collaboration.

Team NICE will consider engineering capabilities, team development, community impact, product needs, and willingness to collaborate. Competition results are one factor, but not the only criterion.

As a general rule, each FRC team should submit only one application. A mentor, teacher, or authorized team representative should complete it. Use the language menu at the top of the page to switch languages.
