The **SplineXS adapter** provides a strong, durable connection for FRC mechanisms.

:::note

The **SplineXS adapter** connects SplineXS components to 1/2” rounded-hex components such as gears and pulleys. Its precise fit helps prevent looseness and the resulting loss of performance. Use it in drivetrains, gearboxes, and other heavily loaded assemblies that need a secure, reliable connection.

:::
