Tube plugs fit inside square or rectangular aluminum tubing. They let you join two tubes or fasten a tube to a panel.

FRC teams commonly build structures from tubing and sheet material. Traditional ways to join these parts include:

* **Welding** — requires equipment and skills that not every team has.
* **Gussets and brackets** — can be awkward in tight spaces or complex assemblies.

**Tube plugs** offer a simple, flexible connection without specialized tools.

## How to Use

#### Application 1: Joining Two Tubes

Tube plugs can join two tubes directly, without an additional plate.

<Tabs>

<TabItem label="Long through-bolts">



<DocImage src="/assets/docs/hardware/tube_and_tube_long_screw.png" alt="Joining tubes with long through-bolts" width={375} />

Pass two long M5 screws through the tube.

* Simple arrangement
* Requires longer screws

</TabItem>

<TabItem label="Short screws">

<DocImage src="/assets/docs/hardware/tube_and_tube_short_screw.png" alt="Joining tubes with short screws and access holes" width={375} />

Enlarge the holes on one side to 10mm, then fasten the joint with two short screws.

* Uses commonly available screw lengths
* Requires enlarging the access holes

</TabItem>

</Tabs>

#### Application 2: Attaching a Tube to a Panel

<DocImage src="/assets/docs/hardware/board_and_tube.png" alt="Tube plug joining tubing to a panel" width={375} />

Tube plugs also provide a direct connection between tubing and sheet material.
