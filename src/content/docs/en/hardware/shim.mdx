A small amount of play in a hex interface can result from normal manufacturing tolerances. Over time, this backlash can cause movement, reduce positioning accuracy, and accelerate wear on the shaft and bore. **Hex-bore clearance shims** take up that fit clearance so the mating parts sit more securely, improving rigidity and control consistency.

## **Benefits**

* **Less backlash** — reduce play for more consistent positioning and response.
* **Greater rigidity** — reduce lost motion and elastic deflection.
* **Reduced wear** — limit uneven wear on the shaft and bore.
* **Adjustable fit** — stack shims to suit the clearance in your assembly.

## **Suggested Applications**

* Pivot shafts
* Robot arm joints
* Shooter angle adjustment mechanisms
* Hex interfaces in gears and sprockets
* Heavily loaded rotating assemblies

:::caution

**Engineering tip:** Use hex-bore clearance shims when assembling hex-bore transmission parts to improve fit and mechanism stability.

:::
