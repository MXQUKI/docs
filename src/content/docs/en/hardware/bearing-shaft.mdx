Shafts carry power through a mechanism. NICE rounded-hex shafts build on the familiar hex profile with several practical improvements:

**Features:**

* Hollow center reduces weight and allows the ends to be tapped
* Rounded corners fit round-bore bearings without aligning the shaft flats
* 7075-T6 aluminum alloy combines strength with low weight
* Smooth black anodized finish helps reduce friction
