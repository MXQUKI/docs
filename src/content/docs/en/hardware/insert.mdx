3D printing is increasingly used to make pulleys, gears, and other power transmission parts. However, printed parts can have several limitations:

* Limited torque capacity
* Deformation around areas of concentrated stress
* Inconsistent dimensional accuracy

NICE 3D printing inserts address these issues with a 6061-T6 aluminum body weighing just 3.4g. The insert reinforces the printed part and helps distribute stress around the shaft interface.

## How to Use

#### Installing an Insert in a Pulley

<DocImage src="/assets/docs/hardware/Insert_wPulley.png" alt="Insert fitted inside a printed pulley" width={375} />

The insert helps prevent cracking and deformation caused by concentrated stress, while allowing the pulley to be printed with substantially less infill.
