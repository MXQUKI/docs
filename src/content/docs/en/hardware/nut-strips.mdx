Nut strips are aluminum bars with a series of threaded holes along their length. They provide multiple fastening points for your structure in one easy-to-handle part.

Nut strips are particularly useful for:

1. **Joining perpendicular panels** — connect two panels at a right angle.
2. **Attaching panels to tubing** — place the nut strip inside the tube to secure the panel.
3. **Flexible fastening positions** — use the available threaded holes instead of positioning individual nuts.

## How to Use

<DocImage src="/assets/docs/hardware/board_and_tube%20%281%29.png" alt="Nut strip joining a panel and tube" width={375} />

To install:

1. Place the nut strip behind the panel or inside the tube.
2. Install screws through the mounting holes into the strip.

:::note

* **Easy alignment** — a series of threaded holes saves you from positioning individual nuts.
* **Cut to length** — trim the strip to suit your assembly.
* **Faster assembly** — no loose nuts to hold in place.

:::
