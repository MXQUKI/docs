## Pre-Drilled Aluminum Tubing

<DocImage src="/assets/docs/structure/2550.png" alt="2550 pre-drilled aluminum tubing" width={375} />

Pre-drilled aluminum tubing makes robot frames quick to assemble. The holes are already cut in a precise pattern, so you can bolt sections together without measuring, marking, and drilling each hole yourself.

### Why Choose Pre-Drilled Tubing?

**Common challenges with plain tubing:**

* **Drilling your own holes** requires suitable equipment and careful setup to keep holes aligned.
* **Starting from plain stock** means measuring and marking each new part.
* **Mixing brands** can introduce incompatible hole patterns.

**With pre-drilled tubing:**

* **Ready to assemble** — the holes are already precisely cut.
* **Consistent pattern** — all sizes share the same hole layout.
* **Quick construction** — cut to length and bolt together.

### NICE Pre-Drilled Aluminum Tubing

* 6061-T6 aluminum alloy
* Laser-cut holes with accurate positioning and smooth, burr-free edges
* Three rows of holes on a consistent 12.5mm pitch
* Clearance holes for standard M5 screws
* 1000mm stock length; cut to suit your design

:::note

All NICE pre-drilled tubing uses the same hole pattern, allowing you to:

* Combine different tube sizes
* Change wall thickness without changing the mounting layout
* Assemble structures efficiently with NICE tube plugs, nut strips, and other components

:::
