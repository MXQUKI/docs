**XPE bumper foam** is designed for competition robot protection. The high-density foam combines low weight with resilient cushioning to absorb collision energy and reduce the risk of damage to the chassis and structure. Its industrial adhesive backing simplifies bumper assembly. Designed for repeated contact and extended use in FRC, the foam maintains its cushioning and shape, whether fitted across the front, sides, or other impact-prone areas of the robot.

## **Why Use XPE Bumper Foam?**

* **Improved durability** — better compression resistance than ordinary PE pool noodles, with less collapse and deformation.
* **Better impact absorption** — reduce the shock transmitted to the chassis and structure.
* **Less deformation after repeated impacts**
* **Consistent cushioning over extended use**
* **Stable performance throughout the season**
* **Suited to high-contact competition environments**

## **Test Results**

In testing after 5 high-intensity impacts:

* **XPE foam showed over 70% less permanent deformation** than ordinary pool noodle foam.
* **More effective cushioning** at the moment of impact
* Reduced impact loading on the robot structure
